1. Architecture Status: Single-Slope CIS Readout

Analog State: CONDITIONAL GO. Photodiode current sinks from a 2.0 pF passive
capacitor (discharge-mode). Chopped/auto-zeroed preamp at 30 uA tail current +
StrongARM latch. Total measured surrogate noise is 53 uV (+/-16% model error
band of 44.5 to 61.5 uV), safely clearing the 72 uV failure point for 56 dB
SNDR.

Digital State (RTL): VERIFIED. 12-bit Gray counter at 6 GHz (carry-select to
pass SS/125C), 4-phase capture (24 GS/s). 3-point digital quadratic
calibration. Icarus Verilog is 10,000/10,000 bit-exact against the Python
golden model.

Energy: Digital logic = 0.04 fJ/MAC. Total readout = 0.55 fJ/MAC (36x below the
20 fJ/MAC budget).

Standing Item: Requires a commercial transient-noise histogram (Cadence
Spectre) to eliminate the +/-16% open-source BSIM4 surrogate uncertainty.

5. AI Onboarding -- gcb-spec/ai-agents/  (introduced at tag v0.1.1)

Role-specific system prompts to initialize AI sessions into isolated personas
so context (and this state) is loaded once, not re-derived every session. Any
new AI agent: read gcb-spec/ai-agents/README.md first and adopt a persona.

  AGENT_01_SYSTEM_ARCHITECT  macro-architecture, Vortex RISC-V ISA tap, DMA /
                             carry-save streaming, MMIO usability, avoiding
                             Von Neumann starvation. Not analog, not RTL.
  AGENT_02_ANALOG_EVALUATOR  deep-submicron physics, SPICE, transient noise,
                             V=Q/C, the CONDITIONAL GO boundary and gate
                             limits. Rejects V=Q/C violations and optical
                             time-expansion delay lines. Not RTL, not arch.
  AGENT_03_RTL_VERIFIER      bit-exact verification, SystemVerilog, static-CMOS
                             timing closure, toggle-count energy, regen.py
                             discipline, DD-001 (carry-select; a 12-bit ripple
                             carry is ~195 ps at SS/125C and fails the 166.7 ps
                             period). Not analog, not arch.

Each prompt embeds this state compression plus its lane's hard-won history and
ends any boundary-crossing analysis with an explicit hand-off line. gcb-spec
README.md has an "AI Onboarding (Paved Road)" section at the top.
